Artboard Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Artboard app ("Artboard", "the app", "we", "us") handles information. Artboard is a drawing and step-by-step drawing learning app for Android phones and tablets, published by DevPress.

Summary

  • Artboard has no accounts, no sign-up and no cloud storage.
  • Your drawings, gallery, settings and learning progress stay on your device.
  • Artboard itself does not collect, upload or sell your personal data.
  • The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect device information to show and measure ads, as described below.

Information stored on your device

Artboard stores the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Your drawings, layers and gallery
  • Brush presets, colour palettes and app settings
  • Tutorial progress and remaining learning turns

We do not have access to this information. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Photos and files

  • Opening a photo: if you choose to open a photo to draw on, you pick it with the system file or photo picker. Artboard only reads the photo you pick, and only on your device.
  • Exporting: when you export a drawing (PNG, JPG, WEBP or PDF), Artboard saves it to your Pictures folder or to a folder you choose. On Android 9 and older, this requires the storage permission. Exported files are not sent anywhere.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Artboard shows banner ads on some screens (Home, Learn and Gallery) and offers optional rewarded ads that grant extra learning turns. The drawing board and tutorials do not show ads. Ads are only loaded when your device is online.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (for example, the Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device type, operating system, language and app information
  • Ad interactions (for example, views and taps)

This data is collected by Google, not by us. Google's use of it is governed by Google's policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google advertising: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Artboard asks AdMob to show only general-audience (rated G) ads.

Your choices about ads

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: Artboard uses Google's consent tool to ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. If consent is not given, ads may be limited or not shown at all.
  • Personalised ads: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (on Android: Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Security & privacy > Privacy > Ads).
  • Offline use: if your device is offline, no ads are requested. All drawing and tutorial features keep working.

Information we do not collect

Artboard does not:

  • Require or create an account
  • Collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts
  • Upload your drawings or photos to any server
  • Use analytics or crash-reporting services
  • Use your precise location, camera or microphone
  • Sell your personal information

Data sharing

We do not share personal information with anyone. The only third party that receives data from the app is Google AdMob, as described in the Advertising section above.

Data security

Your drawings and settings stay in the app's private storage on your device, protected by your device's security. Network connections made by the advertising SDK use encryption (HTTPS); the app does not allow unencrypted network traffic.

Data retention and deletion

Because we do not collect your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server. You can delete your data at any time by:

  • Deleting drawings from the in-app gallery
  • Clearing the app's data in your device settings
  • Uninstalling the app

Files you exported to your Pictures folder or another folder remain there until you delete them.

Children

Artboard is designed to be family-friendly and is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Artboard itself collects no personal information from any user, and it requests only general-audience ads. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child's information has been collected through ads in the app, please contact us and we will help you resolve it with Google.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EEA/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Since Artboard does not collect personal information, most requests can be handled on your device as described above. For advertising data, use Google's controls listed above. You can also contact us with any questions.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when the app adds features. We will post the updated policy at this page and change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
