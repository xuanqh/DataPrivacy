Privacy Policy for Document Reader

Effective date: September 20, 2026
App: Document Reader (Android package com.devpress.documentreader)
Developer: DevPress
Contact: qhxuan@gmail.com

The short version

  • Your documents stay on your device. Document Reader has no accounts, no servers of its own, and never uploads your files, their names, or their contents.
  • The app does not use analytics or crash-reporting services of its own.
  • The app is supported by ads from Google AdMob. Google's ads SDK collects certain device and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads. This is the only routine data flow to a third party, and it never includes your documents (see Advertising).
  • You can remove everything the app stores from within the app or by uninstalling it.

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy describes how Document Reader ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use it on Android. By using the app, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. Information that stays on your device

Document Reader is a local-first reader for PDF, Word (DOCX), Excel (XLSX), PowerPoint (PPTX), CSV, TXT, and EPUB files. To provide its features, the app stores the following only on your device, in its private app storage:

What Why Where it goes
Your Recent and Favorites lists: file name, location reference (path or URI), file size, format, and the time you last opened it To show your recent and starred documents on the Home, Recent, and Favorites screens Stays on the device
Bookmarks you create (a label and a position such as a page, slide, or row) To return you to a place in a document Stays on the device
Preferences: theme, reading theme, text size, line spacing, app language, and opt-in toggles To remember your settings Stays on the device
A temporary copy of a file you open from another app ("Open with", for example from a file manager or an email attachment) To read the file when the source does not give the app direct access Stays in the app's private cache; see Retention and deletion

The app opens files you choose through Android's system file picker (Storage Access Framework) or through "Open with". It does not request broad storage permissions and does not scan your device for documents.

Document contents are processed on your device to display them, search inside them, and share them when you ask. Contents, file names, and file paths are never written to logs and never sent to us.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect or receive:

  • your documents, their contents, names, or paths;
  • your name, email address, phone number, or any account information (there is no account);
  • your precise or approximate location;
  • your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data;
  • usage analytics or crash reports through our own tooling.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Advertising

The app is free and is supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). Ads are shown as:

  • banner ads on the Home and Favorites screens; and
  • an optional rewarded video ad, which you can choose to watch to unlock the "Reading theme" feature.

When the ads SDK is active, Google may collect and process data such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and operating-system information, app version, general ad-interaction data (impressions, clicks), and diagnostic data, to deliver, measure, and secure ads and to prevent fraud. Google may use this to show personalized ads where you have allowed it. This processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

The ads SDK never receives your documents, file names, file paths, or extracted text. Ad placements in the app are identified only by fixed labels that do not depend on what you are reading. The app is designed so that ads never block or interrupt opening or reading a document.

Consent and control

  • In regions where the law requires it (such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before requesting personalized ads. If you decline, or if consent cannot be determined, only non-personalized ads are requested.
  • On Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization at any time in Settings → Google → Ads (the path may vary by device).
  • On Android 13 and later, the app declares the advertising-ID permission that the ads SDK uses. If you delete your advertising ID in system settings, the SDK receives a blank value.

5. Optional features that use the network

The first three items below are off by default or need your explicit action. The last one never leaves your device.

  • Load images from web links (Settings, off by default). Some PowerPoint slides reference a picture by a web address instead of embedding it. If you turn this option on, the app downloads such images directly from the address written in the presentation. The server that hosts the image can see your IP address and that a request was made. When the option is off, the app shows a placeholder and makes no request.
  • On-device OCR (preview, opt-in). Text recognition runs entirely on your device using Google ML Kit's on-device recognizer. Images and recognized text are not uploaded, and temporary image files created for recognition are deleted immediately afterwards.
  • AI document summary. This is not available in this version of the app. If a future version adds a feature that sends document text to a provider, it will be opt-in, will state what is sent and why, and this policy will be updated before it is released.
  • Local viewers. To render PowerPoint and Excel files, the app briefly runs a small server on your device's loopback address (127.0.0.1) that only the app's own viewer can reach. It does not accept connections from outside the device and does not use the internet.

Sharing a document uses Android's share sheet. What you share, and with whom, is entirely your choice and is handled by the app you pick.

8. Security

The app is a reader and does not edit your original files. Office files (DOCX, XLSX, PPTX) are treated as untrusted input: the app validates file types and sizes, limits resource use when unpacking, never runs macros or embedded programs, and does not execute scripts contained in a document. The app's data is stored in Android's app-private storage, and Android backup of app data is disabled. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Retention and deletion

  • Recent, Favorites, and Bookmarks are kept until you remove them. In the app you can remove a single entry, or use Settings → Storage → Clear Recent / Clear Favorites. Clearing these lists never affects your original files.
  • Temporary copies from "Open with" are kept in the app's private cache so the document can be read. You can clear them with Android Settings → Apps → Document Reader → Storage → Clear cache. Android may also remove cached files on its own when it needs space.
  • Everything the app stores is deleted when you uninstall it, or when you choose Clear storage in the same Android Settings screen.
  • Advertising data held by Google is retained and controlled according to Google's policies. Use your Android advertising settings (see Advertising) to control it.

We hold no server-side copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

10. Children's privacy

Document Reader is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your country, such as 16 in parts of the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help address it. Ad-related data is handled by Google as described above.
